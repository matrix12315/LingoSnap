# Surface brief — Translation popup (hero) + Manager

Mode: **Operate** (the visitor completes a task: read a trusted analysis of their
selection, copy it, move on). Scanability and quiet craft outrank expression;
the brand lives in precise typographic details.

Note: the impeccable engine binary could not be fetched (no network for the
release download), so `concept-seed` / `serve-question` did not run. The
direction roll below was performed manually against the new-work playbook and
the build is code-led; the user may re-roll by asking.

## Direction roll (seven candidates from the bilingual reading world)

1. **Print dictionary entry** (OED-style sense record) — authoritative, but it is
   the cream-plus-serif calibration default; declined as the category costume.
2. **Galley proof with proofreader's marks** — the error-check ritual made
   visible. *Competitive*: donates the reference-mark discipline (＊ † ‡ §) and
   the red-review-ink accent.
3. **Loeb Classical Library facing page** — original and translation bound as
   one spread. *Competitive*: donates the pairing contract (target and result
   always typographically paired, never two anonymous boxes) and the spine rule.
4. **Subtitle caption plate** — legible but too thin to carry a whole system; declined.
5. **Interpreter's notepad (A-B notation)** — craft, but its notation is illegible
   to users; a costume of the tool; declined.
6. **Split-flap departure board** — travel metaphor, mechanical mono; wrong
   register for "delicate"; declined.
7. **Letterpress correspondence card — the translator's fair copy** — the
   finished sheet a translator delivers: fine paper, engraved-level hairline
   discipline, one review-ink accent, a seal to close the copy. **Assigned
   direction.**

Raises applied to the assigned direction: the reference-mark system and
review-ink accent from (2); the target/result pairing contract and spine rule
from (3).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The popup is the translator's fair copy — a single sheet of fine paper
laid over the user's work, typeset with the discipline of letterpress stationery;
the incumbent's dark utility chrome is refused entirely (no dark panels, no pill
buttons, no eyebrow labels). OWN-WORLD: warm-white paper ground, warm near-black
ink, one vermilion review ink (seal red), 1px hairline rules, Georgia serif for
quoted text and headings, Segoe UI for controls, Cascadia mono for IPA and IDs,
SimSun for CJK body; a vermilion seal mark is the product signature. STORY: the
visitor sees their selection quoted like a manuscript line, the analysis set
beneath it like a fair copy with a translator's reference marks, and trusts it
because it reads like print, not like a chat bubble; they copy and return to
work. FIRST VIEWPORT: a 448px paper card floating with layered soft shadows on
the desk — top row: vermilion seal (译) + wordmark + status, hairline pin/close
icons; then the quotation (target set in Georgia with hanging punctuation,
context beneath a hairline in muted ink); then the result manuscript with
reference-marked sections (译 Translation / ＊ Words / † Idioms & Grammar /
§ Error check) with IPA in vermilion mono; footer: ink-filled Copy primary,
hairline ghost Retry / Prompt / Pin. FORM: letterpress correspondence card,
candidate 7, raised by 2 and 3 (manual roll; no seed key printed — engine
unavailable). FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build
ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster
carrying its provenance.

## Memorable moment

When the streamed result completes, a small vermilion seal stamps down beside
the final section (scale-and-settle, respects `prefers-reduced-motion`) — the
fair copy is closed. While streaming, a thin vermilion ink caret blinks at the
insertion point.

## Constraints carried from PRODUCT.md

System font stacks only; compact utility density (4px base, 8/12/16/24 rhythm);
body text ≥ 4.5:1 on paper; focus rings visible; every status also stated in
text; popup must remain legible floating over both light and dark host apps.

## Unresolved decisions

Whether the manager adopts the paper world fully or keeps a quieter neutral
shell (decision: full paper world — one visual language, per DESIGN discipline;
"quiet Manager, expressive popup" is achieved by density, not by a second look).

User feedback 2026-10-05 (applied): the chooser must keep the original's
horizontal profile-rail shape — the vertical card list was rejected; the popup
status line carries no "Resident" prefix; Settings gains Interface language and
Theme (Dark/Light) selects in Defaults. The Fair Copy dark variant is a derived
theme from the same tokens — the paper world is the reference theme. The manager
titlebar reads "Selection Translate — Manager" to match the real window title.

Liquid glass (user request 2026-10-05, referring to
`D:\pythonProject\2026\liquid-glass-studio`): the action buttons are liquid
glass, translated into the paper world rather than pasted from the demo —
warm-graded translucent glass (never cool blue-white), functional
backdrop blur+saturate, specular top inset, inner bottom shade, hairline ring,
neutral lift, pill radius rhyming with the profile rail. Harmony rule: chrome
is glass, content is paper — every action button (primary smoked-ink glass,
danger seal-tinted glass, copied ledger glass) is liquid; fields, the chooser
rail, and the manuscript stay paper. A faint warm fiber texture (feTurbulence,
~4.5% alpha) sits under the paper color so the blur refracts real material.
